NaijaHelp Trust Center

v0.9 introduces the trust layer needed for a two-sided services marketplace.

Provider verification

Providers can submit verification documents. Admins review each document and can approve or reject it.

Reviews

Customers can review completed bookings with a 1–5 rating and optional comment.

Reports & disputes

Customers/providers can submit reports. Admins can move reports through Open → Under Review → Resolved/Dismissed.

Admin audit trail

Important moderation actions are recorded for accountability.
